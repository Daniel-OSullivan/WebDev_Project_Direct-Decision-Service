About Wheels on Fire

Hi, I'm John O'Reilly founder, engine enthusiast, and self-proclaimed perfectionist especially when it comes to cars, less so with people - no offense Wheels on Fire - it isnt just a name - it is the result of decades spent under hoods and behind wheels, with a goal of chasing down the perfect growl of a well-tuned engine. Ive been in the car business longer than i care to admit, and if theres one important thing Ive learned, its this: theres a car for everyone - even if you dont know it yet. This site is where I share my heavily curated personal collection, offer handpicked vehicles for sale, and maybe crack a smile (or groan) with my somewhat witty and dry humour along the way. Every car here has been thoroughly vetted by yours truly - because if it doesn't meet my standards, it doesnt get on the site. Simple as that.

Where We're Headed

Wheels on Fire is more than just a showcase - it's the start of something bigger. Im building a place where car lovers, first-time buyers, and seasoned collectors can all find something that makes them stop and say, “Now thats a car.” The plan is to expand the inventory, introduce a search tool that doesnt make you want to throw your phone across the room, and eventually open a showroom thats part garage, part gallery. Think fuel, steel, and a bit of attitude - just like the cars I sell.

How I Treat My Customers

Lets keep this simple: I treat customers the way Id want to be treated - with honesty, no pressure, and a healthy respect for their time (and intelligence). I dont do sales pitches. I do straight talk, good cars, and fair deals. Whether youre browsing or buying, Ill be here to help - not hover. And if you're not sure what you're looking for? Thats half the fun. Ill ask a few questions, offer real advice, and point you in the direction of something that fits your life, not just your budget. So, whether you're looking for your next ride, a classic to fawn over, or just want to admire what a lifetime of obsession looks like - welcome. Kick the tires (figuratively, please), browse the lot, and lets find the car that puts fire in your wheels and your heart.

- John
