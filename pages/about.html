About Wheels on Fire

Hi, I'm John O'Reilly founder, engine enthusiast, and self-proclaimed perfectionist especially when it comes to cars, less so with people - no offense Wheels on Fire it isnt just a name - it is the result of decades enginneering, tinkering, and a few too many late nights spent under the hood. I have always had a passion for cars. longer than i care to admit, and if theres one important thing Ive learned, its this: theres a car for everyone - even if you dont know it yet. This site is where I share my heavily curated personal collection, offer handpicked vehicles for sale, and maybe crack a smile (or groan) with my somewhat witty and dry humour along the way. Every car here has been thoroughly vetted by yours truly - because if it doesn't meet my standards, it doesnt get on the site. Simple as that.

How I Treat My Customers

Lets keep this simple: I treat customers the way Id want to be treated - with honesty, no pressure, and a healthy respect for their time. I dont do sales pitches. I do straight talk, good cars, and fair deals. Whether youre browsing or buying, Ill be here to help - not hover. And if you're not sure what you're looking for? Thats half the fun. Ill ask a few questions, offer real advice, and point you in the direction of something that fits your life, not just your budget. So, whether you're looking for your next ride, a classic to fawn over, or just want to admire what a lifetime of obsession looks like, welcome to Wheels on Fire. Lets find your next car together.

- John
