About Wheels on Fire

Hi, I'm John O'Reilly — founder, engine enthusiast, and self-proclaimed perfectionist (especially when it comes to cars, less so with people — no offense).

Wheels on Fire - it isn’t just a name — it is the result of decades spent under hoods and behind wheels, with a goal of chasing down the perfect growl of a well-tuned engine. I’ve been in the car business longer than i care to admit, and if there’s one important thing I’ve learned, it’s this: there’s a car for everyone — even if you don’t know it yet.

This site is where I share my heavily curated personal collection, offer handpicked vehicles for sale, and maybe crack a smile (or groan) with my somewhat witty and dry humour along the way. Every car here has been thoroughly vetted by yours truly — because if it doesn't meet my standards, it doesn’t get on the site. Simple as that.

Where We're Headed

Wheels on Fire is more than just a showcase — it's the start of something bigger. I’m building a place where car lovers, first-time buyers, and seasoned collectors can all find something that makes them stop and say, “Now *that’s* a car.”

The plan is to expand the inventory, introduce a search tool that doesn’t make you want to throw your phone across the room, and eventually open a showroom that’s part garage, part gallery. Think fuel, steel, and a bit of attitude — just like the cars I sell.

How I Treat My Customers

Let’s keep this simple: I treat customers the way I’d want to be treated — with honesty, no pressure, and a healthy respect for their time (and intelligence). I don’t do sales pitches. I do straight talk, good cars, and fair deals.

Whether you’re browsing or buying, I’ll be here to help — not hover. And if you're not sure what you're looking for? That’s half the fun. I’ll ask a few questions, offer real advice, and point you in the direction of something that fits your life, not just your budget.

So, whether you're looking for your next ride, a classic to fawn over, or just want to admire what a lifetime of obsession looks like — welcome. Kick the tires (figuratively, please), browse the lot, and let’s find the car that puts fire in your wheels and your heart.

— John
